Design Specifications

  • Concept: Footprint/step symbol + coin/reward element
  • Style: Minimalist, modern, iOS-compliant
  • Colors: Primary Blue (#007AFF) + Gold (#FFD700)
  • Requirements: Must be recognizable at 16px

Concept Elements

  • 🦶 Footprint: Represents step tracking and walking activity
  • 🪙 Coin: Represents rewards and "Bolts" currency
  • ⚡ Lightning: Symbol for "Bolts" and energy/motivation
  • 🎯 Integration: Elements should work together harmoniously
